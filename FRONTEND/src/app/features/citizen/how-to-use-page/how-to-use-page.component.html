Welcome to the Digital Grama Niladhari Service Management System.

This system helps you access Grama Niladhari services online in a simple and convenient way. You can submit service requests, upload documents, check request progress, book appointments, contact your GN Officer, and download issued certificates.

1. Sign In to Your Account

Enter your registered email address and password on the login page.

After signing in successfully, you will be directed to your Citizen Dashboard.

Keep your password private and do not share it with anyone.

2. First-Time Login

When you sign in for the first time, you may be asked to change your temporary password.

Enter your current password, create a new password, and confirm it.

Use the new password for future logins.

3. Forgot Your Password

Select Forgot Password on the login page.

Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your email.

You may receive a One-Time Password, also known as an OTP, to verify your identity.

Do not share your OTP with anyone.

4. Citizen Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick view of your activities.

You can see:

  • Submitted requests
  • Pending requests
  • Approved requests
  • Rejected requests
  • Upcoming appointments
  • Recent messages
  • Notifications
  • Issued documents
  • Announcements
  • Community polls

Use the dashboard shortcuts to access the main services quickly.

5. Your Profile

Open the Profile section to view your personal information.

You may see details such as:

  • Full name
  • NIC number
  • Date of birth
  • Contact number
  • Email address
  • Address
  • Family card details
  • GN Division
  • Assigned GN Officer

Some personal information can be updated directly.

For changes to official records, contact your GN Officer.

6. Change Your Email Address

Open the Change Email option from your profile.

Enter your current password and provide the new email address.

A verification code will be sent to the new email address.

Your email will be updated only after successful verification.

7. Change Your Password

Open the Change Password section.

Enter your current password, new password, and confirm the new password.

Choose a secure password that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess.

8. View Available Services

Open the Services page to view the services available online.

Select a service to read its description, instructions, and required documents.

Available services may include:

  • Family card services
  • Citizen record updates
  • Voter registration
  • Certificate requests
  • Document services
  • Welfare and assistance requests
  • Complaints and general requests

9. Submit a Service Request

Select the required service and complete the application form.

Fill in all mandatory fields and check the information carefully before submitting.

Upload the requested documents where necessary.

After successful submission, you will receive a request reference number.

Keep this reference number for future reference.

10. Upload Supporting Documents

Some requests require documents such as certificates, identification copies, or other evidence.

Upload clear and valid files in the accepted format.

Make sure:

  • The document is readable
  • The correct file is selected
  • The file size is within the allowed limit
  • The document is related to your request

Incorrect or unclear documents may delay the request.

11. Track Your Requests

Open My Requests to view all requests submitted through your account.

Select a request to view:

  • Request reference number
  • Service name
  • Submitted date
  • Current status
  • Submitted information
  • Uploaded documents
  • GN Officer remarks
  • Request history

12. Understanding Request Status

Submitted Your request has been successfully sent and is waiting for review.
Under Review The GN Officer is currently checking your request and documents.
Additional Info The GN Officer requires more information or another document from you. Open the request and follow the instructions provided.
Verified The submitted information or document has been checked successfully.
Forwarded The request has been forwarded to another GN Officer for further processing.
Approved Your request has been approved.
Rejected Your request has not been approved. Open the request to view the reason.
Completed The service process has been completed. Any issued document may be available in your Document Vault.

13. Provide Additional Information

When the GN Officer requests more information, open the relevant request.

Read the message carefully and provide the requested details or documents.

Submit the information as soon as possible to avoid delays.

14. Family Card Services

You can use the system for family card-related requests such as:

  • Adding a newborn
  • Adding a family member
  • Removing a deceased family member
  • Changing the family head
  • Requesting a new family card
  • Removing a family member
  • Moving to another GN Division

Complete the form carefully and provide the correct family and address information.

15. Voter Registration

Eligible citizens can submit a voter registration request online.

Complete the required declaration and upload any requested documents.

The GN Officer will review your information before making a decision.

16. Certificate Requests

You can apply for certificates and official letters through the system.

Available documents may include:

  • Character Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • Temporary Residence Certificate
  • Income Confirmation
  • Death Confirmation Letter
  • Other official certificates

Once the request is approved, the document may be available for download.

17. Welfare and Assistance Requests

You can apply for available welfare and assistance programmes.

These may include:

  • Aswesuma assistance
  • Public assistance
  • Elderly assistance
  • Disability assistance
  • Kidney patient assistance
  • Pregnant mother assistance
  • Housing assistance
The system may show suggestions based on your available information. These suggestions do not guarantee approval. The final decision is made by the responsible Officer.

18. Book an Appointment

Open the Appointments section to view available dates and times.

Select a suitable date and time and submit your appointment request.

Dates that are unavailable, fully booked, weekends, or public holidays cannot be selected.

Your appointment is confirmed only after approval by the GN Officer.

19. Appointment Status

Pending Your appointment request is waiting for confirmation.
Confirmed The GN Officer has accepted the appointment.
Rescheduled The appointment date or time has been changed.
Cancelled The appointment has been cancelled.
Completed The appointment has already taken place.

Always check your latest appointment details before visiting the GN Office.

20. Document Vault

The Document Vault stores your available personal and official documents.

You can use it to:

  • View uploaded documents
  • View issued certificates
  • Download official documents
  • Check document dates
  • Search for a document

Keep downloaded documents in a safe place.

21. Download a Certificate

When a certificate is issued, open the Document Vault.

Find the required document and select the view or download option.

The certificate may include an official signature, stamp, certificate number, and QR code.

22. Verify a Certificate

Certificates issued through the system may contain a QR code.

Scan the QR code using a mobile phone to open the verification page.

The page will show whether the certificate is valid, invalid, or revoked.

Do not accept a certificate that is shown as invalid or revoked.

23. Announcements and Notices

Open the Announcements section to view important notices from your GN Officer.

Announcements may include:

  • Office closures
  • Community programmes
  • Public meetings
  • Service updates
  • Important deadlines
  • Emergency information

Check announcements regularly to stay informed.

24. Public Documents

The Public Documents section may contain:

  • Application forms
  • Circulars
  • Notices
  • Guidelines
  • Other public documents

You can view or download the required document.

25. Community Polls

You may take part in community polls published by the GN Officer.

Select your preferred answer and submit your vote.

Each citizen can vote only once in a poll.

26. Contact Your GN Officer

Use the Chat section to communicate with your assigned GN Officer.

Use the general chat for common questions.

Use the request chat for questions related to a specific service request.

You may use chat to:

  • Ask for clarification
  • Respond to an Officer
  • Discuss a request
  • Send a requested document
  • Receive instructions

Always use clear and respectful language.

27. Notifications

Notifications inform you about important updates.

You may receive a notification when:

  • A request is submitted
  • A request review begins
  • More information is required
  • A request is approved or rejected
  • A certificate is issued
  • An appointment is confirmed or changed
  • A new message is received
  • A new announcement is published

Select a notification to open the related page.

28. Common Problems

Unable to Sign In

Check your email address and password. Use the Forgot Password option when necessary. Contact your GN Officer if your account is inactive.

OTP Not Received

Check your spam or junk email folder. Confirm that the email address is correct. Request a new OTP if the previous one has expired.

Unable to Submit a Request

Check all mandatory fields. Make sure the required documents are uploaded. Correct any errors shown on the form.

Unable to Upload a Document

Check the file type and file size. Make sure the document is clear and not damaged.

Unable to Book an Appointment

The selected date may be unavailable, fully booked, a weekend, or a public holiday. Select another available date.

Certificate Not Available

Check whether the request has been approved and completed. Then check the Document Vault and notifications.

29. Safety and Privacy

To protect your account:

  • Do not share your password
  • Do not share your OTP
  • Log out after using a shared device
  • Upload only genuine documents
  • Do not send your password through chat
  • Report suspicious activity to your GN Officer
Your GN Officer will never ask for your password.

30. Log Out

Always select Logout after completing your work.

This is especially important when using a shared or public computer.

Need Help?

For general assistance, contact your assigned GN Officer using the Chat section.

For help with a submitted request, open the request and use the related request chat.

Include the request reference number and explain the issue clearly.

Never include your password or OTP in a message.

The Digital Grama Niladhari Service Management System is designed to help you access GN services more easily, reduce unnecessary office visits, and receive important updates in one secure location.
